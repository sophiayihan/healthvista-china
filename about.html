About us

Built by people who've lived between healthcare systems.

JadeBridge Medical was founded in 2026 by a team with deep roots in Shanghai's tertiary hospital network and first-hand experience of healthcare in the UK and beyond.

What we believe

Quality surgical care should not depend on which passport you hold or which queue you joined. China operates some of the highest-volume, most technically advanced surgical centres in the world — and yet, for an international patient, accessing them has been almost impossible to navigate alone.

Our job is to make that access as simple, transparent, and humane as planning any other significant journey: a clear price, a named surgeon, a person beside you who speaks your language.

What we are not

We are not a hospital. We are not a doctor. We do not perform treatment or give medical advice. JadeBridge Medical is a medical concierge — your translator, advocate, and coordinator. Every clinical decision is made by licensed Chinese physicians at accredited hospitals.

All surgical treatment is performed by licensed physicians at Class A Chinese hospitals. JadeBridge Medical coordinates, translates, and advocates — it does not provide medical care.

Why this team can do it

  • Direct relationships with chief surgeons across Shanghai's Class A hospitals
  • On-the-ground partners in the UK, UAE, Spain, and Latin America
  • Lived experience navigating both Chinese and Western healthcare
  • A lean, AI-enabled operating model — lower overhead, lower prices for patients
