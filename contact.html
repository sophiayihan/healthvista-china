Before you ask

Quick answers

Does the $150 commit me to anything?

No. It pays for a chief surgeon's written review of your records. If you decide not to proceed, nothing further is owed. If we can't treat you, it's refunded in full.

How do I send records securely?

After you submit this form, we send you a secure encrypted upload link. Please don't email medical records as plain attachments.

What languages can I be served in?
