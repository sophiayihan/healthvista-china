Pricing

Pricing you can actually plan around.

American healthcare hides the bill until after the surgery. We give it to you before you book the flight. Here is exactly how our pricing works.

Fixed quote, contractually binding

The package price you accept is the price you pay. The hospital invoices JadeBridge; you pay JadeBridge once, upfront. No itemised surprises afterward.

$150 refundable consultation

Pay $150 to have a Shanghai chief surgeon review your records. If we can't treat your case, you get every dollar back — no questions asked.

Transparent comparisons

Every price we quote is shown next to what the same procedure costs on the NHS, in private UK clinics, and in the United States.

✓ Included in every package

  • Pre-operative imaging and lab work
  • The surgery and surgeon's fee

The core promise

Why you won't get a surprise bill

One contract, one price, signed before you travel

You review and sign a single package agreement with a line-item breakdown. That figure is fixed. It does not change because a procedure ran long or a clinician you never met joined the room.

The hospital bills us, not you

JadeBridge settles directly with the hospital. You make one payment to JadeBridge. You are never exposed to an itemised hospital invoice in a language and billing system you don't know.

Anything extra is approved by you in advance

If a clinical situation genuinely requires something outside the package, it stops and is explained to you with a price — before it happens, never after.

The $150 review is refundable

If after reviewing your records our surgeon concludes we cannot safely or appropriately treat your case, your $150 is returned in full.
