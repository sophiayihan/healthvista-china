How it works

Six steps. One case manager. Zero guesswork.

Every patient gets a single bilingual case manager who owns the entire journey — from the first email to the 12-month post-op follow-up. Here's what happens.

Submit your records

Upload your medical files (imaging, lab work, recent specialist notes) via our secure portal. This step is free and carries no commitment.

Timing: 10 minutes · Cost: Free

Specialist review in 72 hours

A chief surgeon at a Shanghai Class A hospital reviews your case and provides a written second opinion: treatment plan, likely outcome, and a fixed-price quote.

Timing: 72 hours · Cost: $150, fully refundable if we can't treat you

Fixed-price plan

You receive a contractually binding package quote with a line-item breakdown of everything included. You sign once. No itemised billing after surgery.

Timing: 1–3 days · Cost: Quote is free of charge

Travel arrangements

We handle your invitation letter for visa support, flight guidance, airport pickup, and accommodation booking — for you and, if requested, your travelling companion.

Timing: 2–4 weeks to coordinate · Cost: Included in package

Surgery & in-hospital recovery

Your bilingual case manager is physically with you for admission, surgery, and discharge. Daily rounds, in-room translation for every clinician visit, family communication updates.

Timing: Procedure-dependent (3–10 days typical) · Cost: All inpatient costs included

Aftercare for 12 months

Monthly remote follow-up with your surgeon via secure video call. Your case manager remains your single point of contact for any post-op questions, complication review, or local rehab co-ordination.

Timing: 12 months · Cost: Included in package
