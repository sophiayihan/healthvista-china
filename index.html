FAQ

The five questions we hear most

Is medical care in China actually safe?

Every JadeBridge partner hospital is a Class A tertiary institution — China's highest hospital tier — and most carry JCI (Joint Commission International) accreditation, the same standard used to certify top US and European hospitals. Your surgeon will be a chief or attending physician with a minimum of 1,000 documented cases.

What does "fixed price" actually cover?

Your package quote includes: pre-op imaging and labs, the surgery itself, all in-hospital costs (room, nursing, medication), implants where applicable, and the first follow-up visit. You will see a line-item list before you sign. We do not bill you separately afterwards.

What if something goes wrong?

Every patient travels with mandatory short-term international medical insurance (we'll help you arrange it). All partner hospitals carry malpractice coverage under Chinese law. Our case manager stays with you for 12 months after surgery for any complication management or second opinions.

Will I be able to communicate?

Every JadeBridge patient is paired with a bilingual case manager (English, plus Spanish or Arabic on request). The case manager is physically present at the hospital for admission, surgery, and discharge.

How do I start?

Upload your most recent medical records on our consultation page. Within 72 hours, a Shanghai chief surgeon will give you a written second opinion and a price estimate. The review costs $150 and is fully refundable if we can't treat your case.
