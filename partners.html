For partners

Earn meaningful income helping patients access world-class care.

We're building a network of country partners — people with credibility in their community who can guide neighbours, friends, and clients toward affordable surgery in China.

Who fits

Is this you?

  • You live in the UK, Ireland, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Spain, Mexico, or another JadeBridge focus market.
  • You have professional or community access — clinic staff, expat networks, diaspora groups, patient advocacy communities, immigration consultants, community leaders.
  • You can commit at least 5 hours a week.
  • You care about doing this ethically — guiding patients honestly, not pressuring them.
What you'd do

The role, plainly

  • Raise awareness locally using our ready-made marketing materials.
  • Have first conversations with people curious about surgery abroad.
  • Do a light first screening — is this someone we can likely help?
  • Hand the patient to a JadeBridge case manager, who takes over the medical journey.

You are never expected to give medical advice or quote prices. You are a trusted bridge — the clinical and pricing work stays with us.

Commission model

How you earn

Transparent, tiered, and paid promptly. No targets you'll be penalised for missing.

Per consultation

$30

For every patient you refer who completes the $150 records review — whether or not they proceed to surgery.

Most rewarding

Per surgery

$1,200 – $3,500

30% of JadeBridge's commission on every patient who proceeds to surgery. Paid once the hospital invoice settles.

Regional upgrade

Retainer + territory

After 5 converted patients, you qualify for a monthly retainer and exclusive rights to your region.

Commission figures are illustrative and will be finalised in the signed partner agreement. Earnings depend on procedure value and conversion.
