Knee replacement · UK

"I waited two years on the NHS. Six weeks with JadeBridge."

James · 58 · Manchester · June 2025

James had been on the NHS waiting list for a total knee replacement for 22 months when he reached out. The private quote at home was £14,800; he didn't have it.

He uploaded his MRI on a Wednesday. By Friday a chief orthopaedic surgeon in Shanghai had reviewed the case and confirmed a fixed package quote of $9,800 — implant, surgery, 5-night hospital stay, physio, and 12-month follow-up included.

He flew to Shanghai 5 weeks later. Surgery on day 3, walking on day 4, discharged on day 7. He was back at work in Manchester 6 weeks after that.

The numbers: Surgery package $9,800 · Flights £680 · 7-night extra hotel $620 · Total: ~£8,900 vs UK private £14,800.
IVF · Spain

"A fraction of the price, with a doctor who actually called me back."

Maria · 36 · Madrid · October 2025

After three failed IVF cycles in Spain at €7,000 each, Maria was emotionally and financially exhausted. A JadeBridge partner clinic in Shanghai offered a different protocol at €4,800 per cycle, with longer pre-cycle hormonal work-up included.

Maria and her partner spent 19 days in Shanghai. She speaks no Mandarin; her case manager translated every consultation, every document, and every prescription. The clinic's reproductive endocrinologist took her calls personally when she had questions back home.

The first Shanghai cycle resulted in pregnancy. Her daughter was born in Madrid in July 2026.

The numbers: 1 cycle €4,800 · Flights and 19-night stay €2,300 · Total: ~€7,100 vs €21,000 spent in Spain across 3 failed cycles.
LASIK · UAE

"Booked the trip on a Tuesday, flew Friday, saw clearly Monday."

Ahmed · 32 · Dubai · March 2026

Ahmed had been postponing LASIK for years — the Dubai quote was $5,200 and required two separate trips for screening and surgery. JadeBridge routed him to one of Shanghai's highest-volume eye centres, where the screening, surgery, and follow-up all happen in 72 hours.

He flew Friday evening after work, had screening Saturday morning, surgery Sunday morning, and was reading his phone clearly by Sunday night. Flew home Tuesday morning.

The numbers: LASIK both eyes $1,400 · Flights AED 2,100 · 3-night hotel $420 · Total: ~$2,400 vs $5,200 in Dubai.
